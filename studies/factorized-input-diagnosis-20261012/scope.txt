This is a new read-only diagnosis of the original frozen factorized study, prepared before reading its outcomes through this tool. It hashes exact stored float32 inputs after the published neutral clearing rule, checks whether identical inputs require incompatible masks, and groups saved first-choice outcomes by held operator-pair family. All inputs are pinned by the already public SHA256 manifest. It makes no model, compiler, candidate, native or training calls and measures no performance. The previous studies and their weights/outcomes remain unchanged. Duplicate sibling variants do not create independent tasks. Raw input distinction does not establish that a neural network's pooling/hidden representation distinguishes the same rows, nor that a model can learn the theoretical bound.
